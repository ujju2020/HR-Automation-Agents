## 🏛️ Executive Summary & Problem Overview

Modern People Operations (HR) teams at enterprise companies are crippled by tool fragmentation, manual bottlenecks, and reactive firefighting:
1. **Recruitment Screening Fatigue**: Talent teams drown in hundreds of unstructured resumes per requisition, leading to shallow keyword scanning, unconscious bias, and 45-day hiring cycles.
2. **Onboarding Abandonment & Slow Ramp**: Over 30% of new hires experience disjointed onboarding, lacking clear 30-60-90 day milestones, mentor alignment, and automated IT provisioning.
3. **Silent Burnout & Attrition Shocks**: People leaders discover employee dissatisfaction only after exit interviews; conventional quarterly surveys suffer from low participation and delayed signals.
4. **Subjective Performance Appraisals**: Annual 360 performance reviews are fraught with recency bias, anecdotal evaluations, and a lack of objective competency benchmarking.
5. **HR Helpdesk Overload**: People operations staff spend up to 40% of their day answering repetitive policy queries (leave, insurance, benefits) that could be resolved instantly.

**PulseHR OS** solves this through a synchronized **5-Agent Autonomous Swarm**:
- **🎯 TalentScout**: Multi-vector semantic screening, algorithmic DEI bias audits, and auto-generated interview rubrics.
- **🚀 OnboardPilot**: Personalized 30-60-90 day milestone ramp, mentor matching, and provisioning checklists.
- **📊 PulseSentinel**: Real-time team sentiment telemetry, burnout heatmaps, meeting overload fatigue tracking, and proactive managerial intervention playbooks.
- **📈 Elevate360**: Multi-stakeholder appraisal synthesis, 5-dimension competency radars, and objective Promotion Readiness scoring.
- **💬 PeopleAdvisor**: Multi-turn conversational employee assistance grounded in company handbooks with verified policy citations.
